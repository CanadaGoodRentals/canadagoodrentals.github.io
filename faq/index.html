Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about how Good Rentals works, for renters and for landlords.

For renters

Do you have a list of available rentals?

No. Good Rentals doesn’t keep an inventory of homes. I search the market for options that match your criteria and send them to you.

How much does the service cost?

The complete service is CAD 500: a small advance to start and the balance only when you pay the security deposit and secure your home. You can see every fee on the For Renters page.

Can you guarantee I’ll get the rental?

No one can. The landlord or property manager always makes the final decision. I help you present a strong application when they allow me to take part.

What documents will landlords ask me for?

Renting to newcomers is common in Canada, but landlords want to know they can trust you. They usually ask for proof of your legal status in Canada, proof of income or savings, and references. We go over this on our first call so you arrive prepared.

Which homes do you visit?

Only the options you’re interested in. You choose, and I visit and film the inside, the building and the street, and check that the listing is accurate.

Can I or a family member come to the visits?

Yes. You, a family member or a friend are welcome to join. The fee is the same whether or not someone comes, and whether I found the listing or you sent it to me.

How do I receive the options and videos?

By WhatsApp, email or the channel you prefer.

Which areas do you cover?

Victoria, Saanich, Oak Bay, Esquimalt, View Royal, Langford and Colwood. Central Saanich and North Saanich are available by request, with a travel fee for each visit.

Do you work with specific landlords or agencies?

No. I have no contracts or exclusivity with any landlord, property manager or real estate company. My only interest is that you rent the home you choose.

Do you help with rooms or homestays?

No. I only search for entire, self-contained homes for long-term rentals.

Do you help after I sign the lease?

The service ends when you secure your home. Any issue with the property is handled directly between you and the landlord or property manager. If you need it, I can also pick up your keys for you, as an optional extra.

For landlords

How much does it cost?

15% of one month’s rent: 50% non-refundable before the search starts, and 50% when you sign the lease with a tenant I presented. For example, with a monthly rent of CAD 2,000, the fee is CAD 300: CAD 150 before the search and CAD 150 when you sign the lease.

Who runs the showings?

I coordinate the date and time between you and the applicants. Ideally, you show the home yourself, since you can answer every question about it. If the applicant hasn’t arrived in Victoria yet, the showing can be done online.

Do you run background or credit checks?

I pre-screen applicants based on my experience and the information I collect from them at the start: their reasons for moving and how they plan to pay rent. If you wish, you can also run a credit check or a criminal record check, where applicable.

Who provides the photos and videos?

You can provide them yourself, or I can visit your home to take photos and videos at no extra cost, once you’ve paid the first, non-refundable part of the fee. On the day of the visit, please make sure the home is clean and presentable, with no one inside.

Do you manage the property or collect rent?

No. My service ends when you sign the lease with your new tenant.
