Is this service right for you?

Applicants come from my listings and my network: people moving from other countries, from other provinces, and Victoria residents looking for a new home. What they share is that they want a stable, long-term home.

A good fit if you

  • Rent a basement suite, apartment, condo, townhouse or house long term
  • Don’t have the time, or the patience, to answer dozens of inquiries
  • Don’t have a wide network to share your listing with
  • Prefer that someone talks with applicants first and filters them for you

Not the right fit if you

  • Rent rooms, a homestay or a short-term or vacation rental
  • Need someone to run showings, collect rent or manage the property
  • Expect official background, credit or reference checks to be done for you

How it works

  1. Tell us about your property

    Fill out the short form with the details of your rental.

  2. Confirm the service

    Pay the first half of the fee and the search for tenants begins.

  3. I share your listing

    On Facebook Marketplace, other platforms and my own network, with your authorization.

  4. I answer the inquiries

    I reply to interested people and talk with each one about their reasons for moving and how they plan to pay rent.

  5. I present serious applicants

    You receive their information, and I coordinate the date and time of each showing.

  6. You choose your tenant

    You show the home, make your decision and sign the lease. The second half of the fee is due then.

What I do

Share your listing, answer inquiries, talk with interested people and present the applicants who are serious. I facilitate the information between you and them.

What you do

Provide accurate information about your property and make the final decision.

You can provide the photos and videos yourself, or I can take them at no extra cost once you’ve paid the first, non-refundable part of the fee. On that day, the home should be clean and presentable, with no one inside.

I coordinate the date and time of each showing between you and the applicants. Ideally, you show the home yourself, since you can answer every question about it. If the applicant hasn’t arrived in Victoria yet, the showing can be done online.

About screening

I pre-screen applicants based on my experience and the information I collect from them at the start. If you wish, you can also run a credit check or a criminal record check, where applicable.

What’s not included

I don’t guarantee a tenant, sign leases on your behalf, collect rent or manage the property.
