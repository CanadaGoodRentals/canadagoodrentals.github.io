Do you own a rental property in Victoria?

I help landlords find serious, long-term tenants, especially newcomers who are planning their new life in Victoria. This service is for you if you:

  • Don’t have the time, or the patience, to answer dozens of inquiries
  • Don’t have a wide network to share your listing with

Not the right fit if you

  • Rent rooms, a homestay or a short-term or vacation rental
  • Need someone to run showings, collect rent or manage the property
  • Expect official background, credit or reference checks to be done for you

How it works

I share your listing on Facebook Marketplace, other platforms and my own network, and answer the questions that come in. I talk with each interested person about their reasons for moving and how they plan to pay rent, so I can present applicants who are serious.

I coordinate the showings, and you show the home and make the final decision. If you wish, you can also run a credit or criminal record check, where applicable.

Questions people ask first

Do you have a list of available rentals?

No. Good Rentals doesn’t keep an inventory of homes. I search the market for options that match your criteria and send them to you.

Can you guarantee I’ll get the rental?

No one can. The landlord or property manager always makes the final decision. I help you present a strong application when they allow me to take part.

What documents will landlords ask me for?

Renting to newcomers is common in Canada, but landlords want to know they can trust you. They usually ask for proof of your legal status in Canada, proof of income or savings, and references. We go over this on our first call so you arrive prepared.

Do you work with specific landlords or agencies?

No. I have no contracts or exclusivity with any landlord, property manager or real estate company. My only interest is that you rent the home you choose.

Do you help with rooms or homestays?

No. I only search for entire, self-contained homes for long-term rentals.

What happens if I’m not selected after 8 visits?

An additional fee applies, and extra visits are charged separately. You can see every fee, clearly listed, on the For Renters page.

Do you help after I sign the lease?

The service ends when you secure your home. Any issue with the property is handled directly between you and the landlord or property manager. If you need it, I can also pick up your keys for you, as an optional extra.
